# Privacy Policy – Framezz Research Tool

Last updated: September 2026

- This tool is used only by the Framezz owner for internal market research.
- It reads public, non-personal data via official APIs (Etsy, Pinterest):
  search terms, tags, listing titles and trend statistics.
- It does not collect, store or share personal data of other users.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
- API credentials are stored locally and never published.
